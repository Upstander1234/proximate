# MasterOfYourScopeMode

## Purpose
Key art / banner for the Master of Your Scope mode card and its brief scope
overview screen.

## Intended appearance
Confident, competent, focused. An experienced provider at their chosen level
mid-work — hands on the monitor or drawing up a drug — conveying mastery rather
than onboarding.

## Theme / style
Cooler accent (teal/green) against the dark UI. Illustrative, crisp, a little
more clinical and serious than Zero-To-Hero.

## Important visual elements
- A capable provider working a patient with real equipment (monitor, drug box).
- A sense of precision and control.
- Space for a title overlay.

## Notes for the artist / asset generator
Should feel like the "practice at your level" path. Equipment shown must stay
consistent with the game's scope rules (no paramedic gear on an EMT card, etc.).
