# ZeroToHeroMode

## Purpose
Key art / banner for the Zero-To-Hero guided campaign, used on the mode card and
at the top of its intro beat (the Northwood University "PATROL" hook).

## Intended appearance
Warm, hopeful, slightly stylized campus-at-dawn feel. A lone minimally-equipped
first responder (layperson) on foot with a small belt kit, campus buildings
softly behind. Suggests "start from nothing, rise."

## Theme / style
Amber/gold key light against the dark UI. Illustrative, not photoreal. Friendly
and encouraging — this is the teaching path.

## Important visual elements
- A layperson figure on foot (no ambulance) — walking a patrol.
- Subtle campus setting (Northwood University / PATROL program).
- Room at the top/side for a title overlay.

## Notes for the artist / asset generator
Avoid uniforms that imply full EMS certification; this is a trained-layperson
program. Keep the tone optimistic and grounded.
