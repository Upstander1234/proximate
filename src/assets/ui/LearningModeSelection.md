# LearningModeSelection

## Purpose
The screen shown once, when a new save is created, before the provider-level
picker. It presents the two permanent Learning Modes — **Zero-To-Hero** and
**Master of Your Scope** — and makes clear the choice is locked for the life of
the save.

## Intended appearance
A calm, two-card chooser on the game's dark clinical background. Two large
side-by-side (stacking on mobile) selectable cards, each with a title, a one-
line tagline, a short bullet list of what the mode is, and a small "permanent
choice" caption beneath both.

## Theme / style
Matches the existing UI: near-black background, thin hairline borders, mono
labels in wide letter-spacing, restrained accent colors (amber for the guided
path, a cooler teal/green for the mastery path). Quiet and confident, not gamey.

## Important visual elements
- Two cards: "Zero-To-Hero" (guided, campaign) and "Master of Your Scope".
- A lock glyph + "This choice is permanent for this save" line.
- Selected-state highlight ring consistent with other pickers.

## Notes for the artist / asset generator
Keep iconography minimal and legible at small sizes. No stock medical clip-art.
The two cards should read as equally valid paths, not "easy vs hard".
